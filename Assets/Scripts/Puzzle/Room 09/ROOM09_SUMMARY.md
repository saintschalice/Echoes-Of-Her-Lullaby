## 🎯 ROOM CONCEPT

**Lisa is trapped in master bathroom with Emily at full power. Must solve 4 mirror puzzles to reveal the complete truth about mother's murder-suicide plan. Emily attacks if puzzles aren't solved in time.**

---

## 🪞 4 MIRROR PUZZLES

### **1. Medicine Cabinet (60s)**
- Arrange 6 prescription bottles chronologically
- Years: 1973, 1974, 1975, 1975, 1976, 1976
- Reveals: Mother planning for years

### **2. Bathtub Drain (60s)**
- Remove drain cover, find torn note pieces
- Reassemble 4 pieces into complete suicide note
- Reveals: "Tonight I end this child's suffering and mine - forever"

### **3. Vanity Terror (90s)**
- Arrange 8 diary page fragments chronologically
- Shows mother's descent into madness
- Reveals: Timeline from defiance to final plan

### **4. Evidence Sequence (60s)**
- Arrange 4 evidence items in murder plan order
- Rope → Pills → Knife → Bloody Towel
- Each placement shows flashback
- Reveals: Premeditated murder plan

## 📊 TECHNICAL SPECS

### **Puzzle Mechanics:**
- **Drag-and-drop** system for all puzzles
- **Time limits:** 60-90 seconds per puzzle
- **Failure condition:** Emily jumpscare → Game Over
- **Success condition:** Correct arrangement → Mirror complete

### **Progression:**
- Can solve puzzles in **any order**
- All 4 must be complete to progress
- Door unlocks automatically when all complete

### **Difficulty:**
- Mirror 1: Easy (chronological)
- Mirror 2: Medium (reassemble)
- Mirror 3: Hard (8 pages, 90s)
- Mirror 4: Medium (logical sequence)
